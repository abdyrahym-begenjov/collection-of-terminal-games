=================================================================================
                           🤔  QUIZ — GAME RULES  📯
=================================================================================

This is a multilingual quiz game (supporting English and Russian) that saves your 
user profile, tracks high scores, and challenges you across two distinct gameplay 
modes.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚙️  MAIN MENU AND SYSTEM MODES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you launch the game, you can navigate through the following system modes:
* Game: Launches the configuration setup to start a live match.
• Rules: The mode provides players with a quick guide to the game's mechanics, 
winning conditions, and menu navigation.
* Highscores: In this mode, the data is extracted from the local save file and
the formatted tableau is dynamically displayed. It lists the names of all
registered players next to the accumulated points, which will be neatly
distributed between the Timer and Infinity modes.
* Settings: Allows you to switch the active interface language from
English to Russian at any time and change the name for the permanent first player.
* Exit: Safely closes the game application after a confirmation prompt.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📜  GENERAL RULES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Setup: On your first run, you will be prompted to select your language and enter 
your name. Your name and progression are saved across sessions.
* Answering Questions: For every question, you must type a valid letter corresponding 
to the choices provided (A, B, C, D for English or А, Б, В, Г for Russian). Invalid 
responses will not be accepted, and you will be forced to try again.
* Question Pool: Once a question has been asked, it is removed from the active 
question list for that session so you will not see duplicates.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎮  GAME MODES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### A. Timer Mode
In this mode, you race against a ticking clock to answer a fixed set of questions.
Difficulty Parameters:
	- Easy: 15 questions, starting with 45 seconds.
	- Normal: 25 questions, starting with 60 seconds.
	- Hard: 40 questions, starting with 90 seconds.
	- Dangerous: 40 questions, starting with 90 seconds, featuring a "surprise" 
	penalty mechanism.
Rules & Time Mechanics:
* Answering a question correctly awards you an extra 5 seconds.
* Answering incorrectly penalizes your time by the number of seconds it took you 
to think about that 
specific answer.
* Dangerous Surprise: If playing on "Dangerous" difficulty and you guess wrong, 
there is a hidden "bomb" 
among the incorrect answers. If your wrong answer triggers the bomb, you immediately 
lose an additional 10 seconds.
* Sudden Death: If your correct and incorrect counts are completely tied at the 
end of the question pool, the game gives you one final tie-breaker question to 
determine your ultimate victory or loss.
* Winning/Scoring Conditions: If your time drops to 00:00 or below, it's an immediate 
Game Over.
* If you finish all questions, your rank is determined by accuracy: Perfect scores 
give you "You Win!!!" (3 stars), 70% accuracy gives "Good Work!!!" (2 stars), and 
50% accuracy gives "Weak, but still you're great!!!" (1 star). Anything below or 
equal to 50% triggers a Game Over.
* Final points are calculated using your regular correct answers and remaining 
time, divided by your penalizing mistakes to ensure a balanced leaderboard score.

### B. Infinity Mode
This mode tests your long-term survival rather than speed. There is no timer, 
allowing you to take as much time as you need per question.
Rules & Mechanics:
* You begin the game with 3 Heart ( ❤️  ) lives.
* Every correct answer awards you 1 point.
* Every incorrect answer deducts 1 Heart life.
* End Game: The game ends immediately when you lose all 3 hearts (Game Over) or 
if you successfully clear out the entire question database pool, crowning you the 
"ABSOLUTE CHAMPION!!!". Your accumulated points are saved straight to the leaderboard.
