============================================================
             🕹️  HANGMAN GAME — GAME RULES  📯
============================================================

Welcome to the classic game of Hangman! Test your vocabulary 
and save the stickman before it is too late.

------------------------------------------------------------
🎮  HOW TO PLAY
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Start the game and choose "Game" from the main menu.
2. The computer will randomly select a secret word and 
display it as a row of blank underscores (_).
3. Guess one letter at a time to uncover the hidden word.

------------------------------------------------------------
❌ THE RULES OF GUESSING & ERRORS
------------------------------------------------------------
• ✅  Correct Guess: If the letter is in the word, it will 
  reveal its correct position(s).
• ❌  Incorrect Guess: If the letter is not in the word, it 
  is an error. An extra part of the gallows/hangman will be 
  drawn.
• 🙂  If the entered letter is already in the word, entered 
  more than one letter or you press Enter without entering 
  the letter - this is not considered an error!

------------------------------------------------------------
🏆  WINNING & LOSING
------------------------------------------------------------
• 🎉  You Win: Correctly guess all the letters to reveal the 
  complete word before the drawing is finished.
• 💀  Game Over: Accumulating 10 total errors completes the 
  drawing, resulting in a defeat and revealing the secret 
  word.

------------------------------------------------------------
📊  GAME MODES & MENUS
------------------------------------------------------------
• Game: Launch a new puzzle and try to solve the hidden word.
• Rules: The mode provides players with a quick guide to the 
  game's mechanics, winning conditions, and menu navigation.
• Highscores: Check your lifetime wins versus the computer's 
  wins sorted by round types.
• Settings: Update your player Name or switch the game 
  Language and word banks.
• Exit: Safely close the game application.
