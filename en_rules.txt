=================================================================================
                    🏙️  THE CITIES GAME - OFFICIAL RULES  🕹️
=================================================================================

Welcome to "The Cities Game", a classic terminal word game where players take 
turns naming cities. The core mechanic is simple: each entered city must begin 
with the last letter of the previously named city.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚙️  GENERAL GAME MECHANICS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Letter Exceptions: If a city ends with the letters 'ъ', 'ы', or 'ь', that letter 
  is skipped, and the preceding valid letter is used as the target letter instead.
* Formatting: City names are case-insensitive when typed but will automatically 
  be formatted to Title Case by the game engine.
* Uniqueness: A city cannot be used more than once during a single game session.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎮  GAME MODES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### A. INFINITY MODE (Single Player)
Test your ultimate geographical knowledge and endurance!
* Setup: The game selects a random starting city from the database.
* Lives: You begin with 3 Hearts (❤️).
* Gameplay: 
  - Enter a valid city name starting with the last letter of the current city.
  - Making a mistake (wrong starting letter, invalid city, or a city that has 
    already been used) will deduct 1 Heart.
  - Leaving the input empty will trigger a warning but won't penalize your lives.
* End Condition: The game ends when your hearts reach 0, or if you manage to exhaust 
  every single city in the database to become the Ultimate Champion!
* Performance Tiers:
  - 10+ points:  ⭐
  - 20+ points:  ⭐⭐
  - 30+ points:  ⭐⭐⭐
  - 40+ points:  ⭐⭐⭐⭐
  - 50+ points:  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  - 60+ points:  Absolute Champion!!! 🏆

### B. PARTY MODE (2 - 4 Players)
Compete against your friends in a tactical battle of wits, tracking points and 
utilizing unique special abilities.
* Turn Order: Players roll a virtual 6-sided die at the start. The player with 
  the highest roll plays first, followed by descending order.
* Difficulty Parameters (Target Points):
  - Easy: First to reach 10 points.
  - Normal: First to reach 20 points.
  - Hard: First to reach 30 points.
* Lives & Elimination: Each player starts with 3 Hearts (❤️). Failing to provide 
  a valid city on your turn costs 1 Heart. If a player loses all hearts, they are 
  instantly eliminated from the game.
  - NOTE: Taking last place (being the first eliminated) results in scoring 0 points 
  for that match.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🪅  PARTY MODE SPECIAL ABILITIES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every player starts the party mode match with three powerful abilities. Each ability 
can only be used **ONCE** per game. To trigger an ability, type its name instead 
of a city on your turn:

* BLASTER 🔫
  Target any active opponent to blast them, instantly deducting 1 Heart from their 
  life pool. You cannot target yourself or already-eliminated players.

* GAME PASS 🦘
  Safely skip your current turn without providing a city and without losing any 
  hearts. 

* REPLACEMENT 🦝
  Replaces the current city with a completely new random city from the database 
  that shares the same required starting letter. Excellent for when you are stuck.

⭐ THE PERFECT VICTORY BONUS ⭐
If you win a Party Mode match without using a single ability (Blaster, Game Pass, 
and Replacement all remain intact), your final points for that game are **DOUBLED**!

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📊  LEADERBOARDS & PROGRESS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All points accumulated across Infinity mode and Party mode are saved globally in 
the local database (`base.json`). Check the 'Highscores' menu at any time to see 
the overall standings and match results!
